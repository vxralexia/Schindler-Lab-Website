Welcome to the Schindler group at the University of British Columbia (UBC) in Vancouver.

Our group develops new synthetic organic and organometallic methods that enable the synthesis of biologically active molecules. Current interests include methodologies based on earth-abundant metals, natural product synthesis, and investigating the biological activity of natural products and their analogues.
