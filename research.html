Research

Developing innovative strategies for synthesis & catalysis. Our group develops new synthetic organic and organometallic methods that enable the synthesis of biologically active molecules.

Placeholder descriptions written from the group's publication list — to be replaced with the group's own text and figures.

Earth-Abundant Metal Catalysis

Lewis acid catalysis with inexpensive, abundant metals such as iron, including the development and mechanistic study of catalytic carbonyl–olefin metathesis.

Visible-Light Photochemistry

Visible light–mediated cycloadditions, such as aza Paternò–Büchi reactions, to access strained nitrogen and oxygen heterocycles including azetidines, azetines and oxetanes.

Natural Product Synthesis

Concise total syntheses of complex, biologically active natural products, often enabled by the methods developed in our group.

Biological Activity

Evaluating the biological activity of natural products and their synthetic analogues.
