Current Research Interests

Developing innovative strategies for synthesis & catalysis. Our group develops new synthetic organic and organometallic methods that enable the synthesis of biologically active molecules.

Carbonyl-Olefin Metathesis (COM)

Lewis acid–catalyzed carbonyl–olefin metathesis using inexpensive, earth-abundant metals such as iron, including the development of new variants and mechanistic studies of these reactions.

Total Synthesis and Natural Products

Concise total syntheses of complex, biologically active natural products, often enabled by the methods developed in our group, and evaluation of the biological activity of natural products and their analogues.

Photochemistry and Heterocycles

Visible light–mediated reactions, such as aza Paternò–Büchi cycloadditions, to access strained nitrogen and oxygen heterocycles including azetidines, azetines and oxetanes.
