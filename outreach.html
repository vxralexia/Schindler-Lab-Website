Outreach

Programs below are from the group's time at the University of Michigan — to be replaced with current UBC outreach.

Many of us became interested in science early on thanks to an inspiring teacher or mentor, and we want to pass that enthusiasm on to young people. Our outreach programs run day camps throughout the year that introduce participants to different areas of science and chemistry — complete with liquid nitrogen ice cream and frozen roses smashed with a hammer.
