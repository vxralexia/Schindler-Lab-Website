Tim McClure

5th Year Graduate Student

B.S., Occidental College

M.Sc., University of Michigan

Originally from Concord, Massachusetts, Tim earned a B.A. in Chemistry from Occidental College in 2022, where he conducted research with Professor Raul Navarro on palladium catalysis and the total synthesis of natural products. He then joined Professor Corinna Schindler’s group at the University of Michigan, earning an M.Sc. in 2024 before moving with the group to the University of British Columbia. His current research focuses on the intersection of electrosynthesis and machine learning for reaction method development.
