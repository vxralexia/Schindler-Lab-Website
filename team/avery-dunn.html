Avery Dunn

1st Year Graduate Student

B.S., Carlton College

Avery grew up in Portland, Oregon, and completed her B.A. in chemistry at Carleton College. There, she worked with Professor Kazimer Skubi to investigate the regioselectivity of radical addition to chalcones. To pursue her continued interest in photochemistry, Avery moved to Vancouver in the fall of 2026 to join the Schindler Lab. Her current work focuses on the development of new methods towards the synthesis of azetidines. Outside of the lab, Avery enjoys backpacking, cooking, and ceramics.
